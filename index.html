Lokeify Multi-store e-commerce platform for fashion brands

Featured · 2026

A Shopify-style platform: merchants sign up, open a store and run it from their own admin, and every store gets a live storefront with a 3D fit room and size advice built in.

  • Merchant admin — sales & orders KPIs with a 30-day revenue chart, product editor (photos, colorways, sizes, stock), orders, customers and newsletter sign-ups.
  • Theme editor — draft → publish flow with reorderable home-page sections, hero banner, drop countdown and trending products.
  • Storefront & checkout — collections, search, product pages, lookbook, bag and customer accounts; the server prices the bag and deducts stock.
  • Fit tech — 3D fit room (Three.js / React Three Fiber) with male and female avatars, a size-recommendation engine and AI photo try-on through swappable providers.
  • Built for serverless — every query scoped per shop, signed http-only cookie sessions, scrypt passwords, SQLite / Turso and chunked uploads on Vercel.
Try the merchant admin: demo@lokeify.com / demo1234
